# Scientific and operational limitations

Custerra is an engineering prototype, not an official warning authority, emergency-response service or validated disaster forecast.

- **Overall risk is UNKNOWN.** Recent earthquake activity and thermal proximity are experimental indicators, not calibrated probabilities of loss, ignition or safe travel.
- **Reported points are not affected areas.** A route near an epicenter or advisory point is not necessarily inside an impact footprint. Road closures, current traffic and arrival-time hazard conditions are not established.
- **Coverage is incomplete.** Missing keys, unsupported hazard families, source outages, recent-feed limits, revision delays and truncated responses can omit relevant evidence. Empty results and absent notices do not establish safety.
- **Thermal detections are not confirmed fires.** Satellite anomalies need source/product context and independent interpretation.
- **Monitoring is bounded.** Lookback windows, polling cadence, provider revisions and partial feeds can miss changes. Incomplete queries cannot confirm that an event disappeared. A first usable source check establishes its baseline.
- **Progress is approximate and optional.** A recent explicit point may select remaining segments conservatively; it is not navigation or continuous tracking. Stale/unmatched points fall back to the full route.
- **Delivery is not guaranteed.** Queueing and push-provider acceptance do not prove receipt or native display. Permission, device/platform behavior, network state and service configuration matter.
- **Offline means saved evidence.** Copies can be stale, expire or be removed by browser cleanup, and are visible to anyone using the browser profile. They do not fetch new warnings or provide offline tile packs.
- **Free hosting can sleep or stop at limits.** It is unsuitable for a dependable continuous warning service. Workers and outbound push are disabled in the free demo.
- **Research remains separate.** A small retrospective weather-label experiment does not establish geographic generalization, causality or advance prediction and does not affect live assessments.

Current release gates include security findings, live FIRMS, native-device notification testing, operational alerting, compatible recovery and independent review. No production rollout is authorized by the documentation. Follow local government and emergency instructions.
